Signal fidelity and interpretation

Model limits

SNR is modelled separately for RF reception, radar echo, acoustic detection and an image-quality proxy. The four SNR values are not directly comparable.

PSNR* is a normalized synthetic reconstruction diagnostic, shown at your request. Here normalized model PSNR* = SNR + 6.02 dB assumes a clean reference with RMS 0.5 and peak amplitude 1. In real systems PSNR requires a reference signal or image; it is not a standard cross-modality performance measure, nor estimated from actual SDR/audio/video recordings here.

Fusion uses nonlinear range and/or direction measurements in a constant-velocity 3D EKF. Quality-adaptive fusion uses observation-specific covariance, and robust fusion additionally downweights or rejects large innovations. Radar is a single hypothetical unit at LW3; microphone arrays are at LW2/LW4; cameras at LW1/LW3. No true radar/SDR raw-IQ generation or detector CNN is claimed.

Research extensions: compare calibration against AERPAW Dataset 28, then collect synchronized microphones and camera observations. See accompanying technical paper and source archive.
